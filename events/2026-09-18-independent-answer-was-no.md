title: The independent answer was no
---

We submitted an exact candidate to the independent certifier.

It came back REVISE.

That was one of the best results the programme could have produced.

The point of an independent gate is not to produce certificates. It is to make the difference between "the builder thinks this is ready" and "someone without ownership of the design could establish that it is ready."

Our candidate did not meet that bar. Neither did several that followed.

A review system that always agrees with the producer is a formatting step. We wanted a control, and the control said no.
