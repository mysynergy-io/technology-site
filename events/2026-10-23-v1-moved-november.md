The countdown moved out thirty-one days. That is the third move, and the two before it are still on this page.

The reason is the same each time. The operating work kept turning up things worth fixing — an approval step that passed without reading anything, a warning we believed we had sent — and we would rather close those than hold a date with a thin page behind it.

Three moves means this date is a forecast, not a promise. We publish it anyway, because a countdown nobody explains is worse than one that slips in public.
