title: The builder does not decide when it goes live
---

Three separate roles now sit between a change and production: whoever builds it, an independent party who approves it, and the owner who decides it goes live.

They answer different questions and they belong to different people.

When we moved a baseline into service this month, the record said exactly what moved and what did not, with the remaining conditions listed open rather than implied closed.

The pull is always to collapse these steps, because approved work feels finished and the last signature looks like paperwork. Collapse them and the person who wanted it to succeed is the person who declares it live.
