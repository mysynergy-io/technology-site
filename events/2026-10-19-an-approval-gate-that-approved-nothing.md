title: An approval gate that approved nothing
---

A release step was meant to refuse anything that had not been approved. It reported pass.

It passed because both halves of its comparison were empty. The approval record was missing, the field it compared against was missing, so the two agreed.

Nothing was approved. Nothing was refused. The control was reporting health on a question it had never managed to ask.

Any check that can pass without having read anything needs a case proving it can fail. Otherwise it is a formality wearing the clothes of a control.
