title: Who may approve our own work
---

Work here cannot go live until an independent party approves it. The rule only means something if the approver's identity cannot be faked.

We tested a forged approver against the live approval control. It was accepted.

The control had already passed independent review, which is what made it worth attacking. Approval is evidence that specific questions were asked, not that every future question will come back clean.

Fixed, re-approved, then activated. Segregation of duties is only as strong as the identity check underneath it.
