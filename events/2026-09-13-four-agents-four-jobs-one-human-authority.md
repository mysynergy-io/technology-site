title: Four agents, four jobs, one human authority
---

We are building an operating model where different AI agents do different jobs on purpose.

One builds. One challenges the design. One certifies the exact result. One can operate the surrounding system. None of them gets the full chain of authority.

The human principal remains the point where certification becomes permission to act.

This is slower than giving one capable agent a broad credential and a long prompt. It is also much easier to reason about. The builder has an incentive to finish. The reviewer has an incentive to find what is wrong. The certifier has no authority to turn its own decision into an effect.

We are not trying to remove people from the loop. We are trying to put the person at the decisions that actually require authority.
