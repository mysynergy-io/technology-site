title: Two independent reviewers, one model
---

We send significant work to two mandatory reviewers before it ships. They are meant to be different systems, so that one produces real disagreement rather than an echo, and so that an outage at one vendor does not take out the whole review chain.

We sent both the same bundle. Both came back having found the same two defects, independently. We were about to quote that agreement as strong corroboration.

Then we read the dispatch metadata. Both were answering on the same model, from the same provider account.

Their agreement was one reviewer answering twice. The gate was structurally sound and evidentially hollow at the same time — the org chart showed two reviewers and the runtime held one. Independence is a property of what is actually answering, not of the job title in the process document, and it has to be measured at the moment of dispatch rather than assumed from configuration.
