title: We withdrew our own candidate before the reviewer touched it
---

We submitted a candidate, then audited our own evidence and found two required falsifiers were missing.

The code might have been correct. That was not the point. We had claimed a level of proof we had not actually produced.

So we withdrew the candidate from the certification path before the reviewer touched it. The submission remains in the record; it was not deleted or rewritten.

The next candidate added the missing attempts to break the design.

One of them found a real defect.

That is why we keep the failed and withdrawn objects. A clean history tells you only what survived. A useful history tells you what we believed, what challenged it, and where the belief changed.
