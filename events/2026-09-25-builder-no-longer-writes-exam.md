title: The builder no longer writes the exam
---

The undefined test exposed a deeper problem.

The producer had written the review prompt. A phrase copied into that prompt became a mandatory certification criterion even though nobody had explicitly authorised it.

That meant the builder could accidentally write the exam its own work would be judged against.

We changed the rule.

Review criteria now have to come from a versioned, ratified mandate. The producer can propose a change and an independent reviewer can challenge it, but the producer cannot make a new certification requirement authoritative by putting it in a prompt.

The mistake stays in the record. The useful part of the mistake is the mechanism we changed so it cannot quietly happen again.
