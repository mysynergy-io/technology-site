title: We gave evidence its own place to live
---

A review that exists only inside a conversation is difficult to audit later.

So we moved the certification exchange onto a durable evidence plane. A candidate is submitted as an exact object. The reviewer returns a structured disposition against that object. Corrections create a new candidate rather than rewriting the old one. Earlier failures stay visible.

The result is a chain you can inspect after everyone has forgotten the details.

That is important for autonomous systems because memory is not governance. If a future agent wants to know why something is trusted, the answer should not be "because the previous session said so." It should be able to find the object, the review and the decision that connected them.
