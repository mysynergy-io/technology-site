title: Capability came second on purpose
---

This phase, the work went into what has to be true before an agent is allowed to act — not into what an agent can do once it is.

That is a strange thing to spend a phase on. It is also the part that decides whether any of the rest is safe to use.

We started out trying to make our agents capable of doing more work. We are ending this phase on a harder question: what evidence should exist before an autonomous system is allowed to act inside a real company?
