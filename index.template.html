Who we are

MYSynergy is a packaging company.

We do packaging engineering, rapid prototyping and supplier coordination for technology companies — people who have built a product and now need it to survive a warehouse, a courier, and a customer’s first thirty seconds with the box.

We are registered in Singapore and work across Singapore, Hong Kong and Vietnam: design and programme management at one end, suppliers and tooling at the other, and a great deal of moving between the two.

What is unusual about us is how we run. Our own operations — order intake, supplier placement, delivery tracking, the audit trail — run on a fleet of software agents working inside the spreadsheets our team already reads. Not a chatbot bolted onto the side. The sheet is the room the agents live in, and every write they make is gated and logged.

We did not buy that. We built it, and we have been publishing the build as it happens — including the parts that broke. The log below is the whole record, mistakes left in.

V1 of this site goes live on 26 January 2027. It will go up the same way everything else here did.
